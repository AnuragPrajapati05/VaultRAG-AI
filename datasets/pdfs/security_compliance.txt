2.1 PRINCIPLE OF LEAST PRIVILEGE
All system access must follow least-privilege principles. Users receive only the minimum access necessary to perform their job functions.

2.2 ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC)
Access is granted based on job role, not individual request:
  - Admin: Full system access
  - HR_Manager: HR systems, attendance, payroll (read)
  - Finance_Analyst: Financial systems, audit reports, budgets
  - Engineer: Infrastructure systems, incident reports, deployment systems
  - Compliance_Officer: All audit logs, compliance documents, GDPR records
  - Employee: Self-service HR portal only

2.3 PRIVILEGED ACCESS MANAGEMENT (PAM)
Production database access requires:
  - MFA (multi-factor authentication)
  - Just-in-time (JIT) access provisioning
  - Session recording and audit logging
  - Automatic 8-hour session expiration

2.4 ACCESS REVIEWS
Quarterly access reviews are mandatory. Managers must certify all team member access rights. Orphaned accounts must be disabled within 24 hours of employee departure.

SECTION 3: INCIDENT RESPONSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Security Incident Classification:
  P1 Critical: Active breach, ransomware, data exfiltration — Response: 15 minutes
  P2 High:     Unauthorized access attempt, policy violation — Response: 1 hour
  P3 Medium:   Security misconfiguration, failed scans — Response: 4 hours
  P4 Low:      Policy inquiry, minor access issue — Response: 24 hours

All security incidents including INC-2045 are logged in security_alerts.json 
and cross-referenced in audit_logs.csv.

ANOMALY DETECTION: Automated detection is in place for:
  - 5+ failed login attempts within 10 minutes from same IP (threshold exceeded during INC-2045)
  - Off-hours privileged access (flagged in access_logs.json)
  - Data exfiltration patterns (>100MB transfers)
  - Geographic anomalies (login from new country)
