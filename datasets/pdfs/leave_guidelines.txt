SECTION 1: TYPES OF LEAVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue PTO at the following rates:
  - Years 0–2:    15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
  - Years 3–5:    18 days per year (1.50 days/month)
  - Years 6–10:   22 days per year (1.83 days/month)
  - Years 10+:    25 days per year (2.08 days/month)

Maximum PTO carryover: 10 days. Excess PTO is forfeited on December 31.

1.2 SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 10 sick days per calendar year, non-accruing and non-transferable. Sick days do not carry over. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a physician's note is required.

1.3 MATERNITY/PATERNITY LEAVE
Primary caregivers: 16 weeks paid leave.
Secondary caregivers: 4 weeks paid leave.
Leave must begin within 6 months of birth/adoption.

1.4 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 paid days.
Extended family (grandparent, in-laws): 3 paid days.

SECTION 2: LEAVE REQUEST PROCEDURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 ADVANCE NOTICE
For planned leave (vacation, personal): Submit request minimum 14 days in advance via the HR portal.
For emergency leave: Notify your direct manager as soon as practicable.

2.2 APPROVAL WORKFLOW
1. Employee submits request via HR portal (portal.vaultcorp.internal)
2. Direct manager approves/denies within 3 business days
3. HR department finalizes and updates attendance records
4. Employee receives email confirmation

2.3 ATTENDANCE TRACKING
All leave is recorded in the employee attendance system (see employee_attendance.csv). Discrepancies must be reported to HR within 5 business days. Attendance data feeds into the quarterly performance review process.

SECTION 3: SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 UNPAID LEAVE
Employees who exhaust all paid leave may request unpaid leave of up to 90 days, subject to manager and HR approval. Benefits continue during the first 30 days of unpaid leave.

3.2 FMLA
VaultCorp complies with the Family and Medical Leave Act. Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for qualifying family or medical reasons.
