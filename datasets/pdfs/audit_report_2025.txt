1.2 HIGH-RISK FINDING #1: ENGINEERING BUDGET OVERRUN
The Engineering department exceeded its Q1 budget by 5.5%. Root cause: emergency procurement of replacement server hardware following the infrastructure failure documented in Incident Report INC-2045 (May 16, 2025). The procurement bypassed the standard three-bid process due to urgency. Recommendation: Establish an emergency procurement protocol with post-hoc audit triggers.

1.3 HIGH-RISK FINDING #2: PAYROLL PROCESSING DELAY
The payroll processing failure on May 16, 2025 (Incident INC-2045) resulted in delayed payments for 847 employees. Manual processing costs totaled $28,400. Legal review is ongoing regarding potential labor law violations. HR and Finance are jointly managing remediation.
