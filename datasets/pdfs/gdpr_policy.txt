SECTION 1: SCOPE AND PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This policy governs how VaultCorp Enterprises collects, processes, stores, and protects personal data in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679 and applicable national data protection laws.

This policy applies to:
  - All VaultCorp employees, contractors, and third-party processors
  - All systems processing personal data of EU/EEA data subjects
  - All company departments including HR, Finance, Engineering, and Sales

SECTION 2: DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Under GDPR, data subjects have the following rights, which VaultCorp must honor:

2.1 RIGHT TO ACCESS — Data subjects may request a copy of their personal data within 30 days.
2.2 RIGHT TO RECTIFICATION — Incorrect data must be corrected within 30 days of request.
2.3 RIGHT TO ERASURE — "Right to be forgotten" — data must be deleted unless retention required by law.
2.4 RIGHT TO DATA PORTABILITY — Data must be provided in machine-readable format (JSON/CSV).
2.5 RIGHT TO OBJECT — Data subjects may object to processing for direct marketing.
2.6 RIGHT TO RESTRICT PROCESSING — Processing may be restricted while accuracy disputes are resolved.

Response SLA: All GDPR data subject requests must be acknowledged within 72 hours and fulfilled within 30 days.

SECTION 3: DATA CLASSIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SPECIAL CATEGORY DATA (highest protection):
  - Health and medical records
  - Biometric data
  - Religious or political beliefs
  - Trade union membership

PERSONAL DATA (standard protection):
  - Name, email, phone number
  - IP addresses, location data
  - Payroll and compensation data
  - Performance review records

PUBLIC DATA (minimal protection):
  - Published job titles and departments
  - General company information

SECTION 4: BREACH NOTIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

In the event of a personal data breach:
  1. Identify and contain the breach immediately
  2. Assess risk to data subjects within 2 hours
  3. Report to Data Protection Authority (DPA) within 72 hours if risk is high
  4. Notify affected individuals without undue delay if high risk

Breach Register: All breaches, including near-misses, must be logged in the 
Compliance Breach Register (maintained by Compliance Officer).

INCIDENT INC-2045 (May 16, 2025): The engineering outage temporarily exposed 
payroll data access logs. Compliance review concluded: no personal data exfiltrated; 
breach notification to DPA not required. Documentation filed in audit_logs.csv.
