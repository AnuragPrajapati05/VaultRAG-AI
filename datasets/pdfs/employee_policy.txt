SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1.1 EMPLOYMENT AT WILL
All employees of VaultCorp Enterprises are employed on an at-will basis unless otherwise specified in a written employment contract. Either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, subject to applicable legal requirements.

1.2 EQUAL OPPORTUNITY EMPLOYMENT
VaultCorp Enterprises is committed to equal employment opportunity for all employees and applicants without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

1.3 PROBATIONARY PERIOD
All new employees are subject to a 90-day probationary period. During this time, performance, attendance, and conduct are closely monitored. The probationary period may be extended at management discretion.

SECTION 2: CODE OF CONDUCT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR
Employees are expected to maintain professional standards at all times. This includes respectful communication, adherence to dress code, and compliance with all company policies. Violations will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

2.2 CONFIDENTIALITY
All employees must sign and adhere to the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) upon joining. Unauthorized disclosure of proprietary information, client data, or internal financials is grounds for immediate termination and potential legal action.

2.3 CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to the HR department immediately. This includes personal financial interests in competitor firms, vendor relationships, or outside employment.

SECTION 3: COMPENSATION AND PAYROLL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 PAYROLL SCHEDULE
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. The payroll processing cutoff is Wednesday midnight before each payday. Any timesheet submissions after the cutoff will be processed in the next cycle.

3.2 PAYROLL SYSTEM OUTAGES
In the event of a payroll system outage (such as the incident recorded under INC-2045 on May 16, 2025), the HR and Finance departments will coordinate to issue manual payments within 72 hours. Affected employees will be notified via corporate email.

3.3 OVERTIME POLICY
Non-exempt employees who work more than 40 hours per week are entitled to overtime pay at 1.5x their regular hourly rate. Overtime must be pre-approved by the department manager.

SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1 ANNUAL REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Ratings range from Unsatisfactory (1) to Outstanding (5). Employees with ratings of 3 or above are eligible for merit increases.

4.2 PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLANS
Employees receiving a rating of 1 or 2 will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 60–90 days. Failure to meet PIP objectives may result in termination.
